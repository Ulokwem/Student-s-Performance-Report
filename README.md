📌 **About the Project**

This project is a Students Performance Dashboard built in Power BI to analyze academic results, demographics, and performance patterns.
It provides an interactive view of student performance, enabling educators and decision-makers to track KPIs, evaluate learning outcomes, and uncover insights across multiple dimensions.

✨ **Features**

🎓 Overall Performance – Monitor total students, gender distribution, and average scores.

📊 Grade Distribution – Visualize how students are spread across letter grades (A–F).

🏆 Top 5 Students – Identify the highest-performing students by average score.

📈 Subject Insights – Track performance across Math, Reading, and Writing.

👨‍👩‍👧 Parental Education Impact – Analyze the effect of parental background on scores.

🥪 Lunch/Test Prep Effect – Discover how socio-economic factors influence academic results.

🌍 Demographic Insights – Compare performance across gender and race/ethnicity.

🎓 **Insights**

📍 Overall Student Overview

     ▪ Total Students: 1,009
     
📍 Gender Distribution: 48.3% Male | 51.7% Female — fairly balanced population.

     ▪ Average Score: 67.8 — indicating average performance across the cohort.
    
📍 Subject Proficiency

     ▪ Reading: 69.1% average

     ▪ Writing: 68.1% average

     ▪ Math: 66.1% average

     ➤ Reading scores are slightly stronger, while Math remains the weakest area.

📍 Grade Distribution

     ▪ Majority of students achieved grades B, C, and D — indicating a mid-level academic performance cluster.

     ▪ Very few students fall in grade A or F, suggesting moderate variation in scores.

📍 Top 5 Students

     ▪ All top performers are female, with average scores ranging from 99.0 to 100.0.

     ➤ Strong indicator of female academic excellence in this dataset.
 
📍 Performance by Race/Ethnicity

     ▪ Distinct performance differences observed between groups (A–E), with Group C and D showing relatively better outcomes.

📍 Impact of Parental Education

     ▪ Students with parents holding bachelor’s and master’s degrees have the highest median scores.

     ▪ Lower performance is observed among students whose parents have only high school education.

 Lunch Impact Analysis

       ▪ Students on standard lunch plans perform significantly better in both Reading and Math compared to those on free/reduced lunch.

       ➤ Economic background may influence academic outcomes.


💡 ** Key Recommendations**

📍 Math Performance Improvement

    ▪ Introduce targeted math support programs and interactive learning tools to close the gap with Reading and Writing scores.
 
📍 Equity Support Initiatives

    ▪ Since lunch type impacts performance, consider nutritional support or tutoring programs for students on free/reduced lunch plans.

📍 Parental Engagement Programs

    ▪ Conduct parent workshops to engage families with lower educational backgrounds — improving student motivation and home learning environments.
    
📍 Recognize and Encourage Female Achievers

    ▪ Leverage the success of top female students through mentorship programs and peer-led tutoring to inspire others.

📍 Race and Inclusion Strategies

    ▪ Identify underperforming racial groups (e.g., Group A or B) and tailor inclusive academic interventions to support balanced performance.

📍 Performance Benchmarking

    ▪ Maintain data tracking of grade distribution trends over time to monitor improvement and evaluate educational initiatives.
